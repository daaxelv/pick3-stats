How CASH POP works

  1. Choose one or more distinct numbers from 1–15, or select all 15.
  2. Choose $1, $2, $5, $10 or $20 per number per draw. You can play 1–20 consecutive draws.
  3. Your ticket assigns a random prize to each number when purchased. One number is drawn; match it to win that number’s assigned prize.

Drawings currently run approximately every 4 minutes, from 5 AM to 2 AM Eastern. Before June 27, 2022 they ran every 15 minutes; compare hit percentages rather than raw totals across these eras.

Prizes range from 5× to 250× the per-number wager: $5–$250 at $1, up to $100–$5,000 at $20. The $20 option began November 4, 2024. There is no shared accumulating jackpot.

A single number has a 1 in 15 (6.67%) chance of matching each draw. Playing all 15 guarantees a matching number, not a profit. Past hits do not change the next draw’s probability. Draw history cannot reveal an individual ticket’s randomly assigned prize.

Hourly share means a number's hits divided by all collected draws during that hour. The difference in percentage points (pp) compares that share with the number's share across the entire selected period. No draws is different from zero hits.

Click a number's table heading to sort by its hourly share; click again to reverse. Best-hour observations require at least 100 collected draws in a bin. Small differences and selected peaks may arise by chance; these observations are for exploratory research.

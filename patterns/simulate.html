2. Set the calendar and cash assumptions

Mock calendar: Powerball Monday/Wednesday/Saturday; M4L daily; Take 5 Midday then Evening daily. Mock drawing numbers are generated independently of the ticket seed streams. Backtests use only actual collected drawings, including their recorded session; missing draws are not filled in.

Cash reports are scenarios under current base-game rules, before taxes. They exclude add-ons, actual historical prize pools, outside winning tickets and liability adjustments. A jackpot cash assumption is split among that portfolio’s own jackpot tickets for the drawing. Take 5 free plays are counted separately, never booked as cash or automatically replayed. Blank variable prizes stay unavailable when hit; they never become $0.

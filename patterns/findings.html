Research settings

Pattern findings

Counts use every collected drawing, including the first 20 used as training in backtests. Patterns can overlap. Common broad patterns cover many possible sets; their count is not evidence that a particular set has an advantage.

Progression means the exact start and step above; Fibonacci means five from 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34, 55 within the game range; spread means two low, two middle and one high; parity uses the selected odd count; above-birthday means all five above 31. Bonus balls are unrestricted except with M4L 04 focus.

Candidate plays

Research score (0–100): each main number’s collected hit count divided by the most frequent main number’s count; average the five values × 100. For Powerball/M4L, also normalize the separate bonus count by the most frequent bonus count, then average all six values. Jersey Cash 5 has no separately selected bonus. This measures individual-number historical frequency within legal pattern samples; it is not a winning probability or proof that the whole combination repeats. Equal scores share a competition rank (1, 1, 3); deterministic number order breaks display ties.

Candidate rankings describe the collected history, using up to 50 seeded sets per pattern. They do not search every possible combination or predict the next drawing. Exact historical matches do not establish ticket purchases or claimed jackpots. Saved plays remain on this browser and device.
